  • Miki Anslow to Connor Quinn

    My work in the Romano household is nearing it's end. I have nearly finished investigating what you had told me to. Sadly, there is nothing that the police may find interesting and would get Giovanni the punishment he deserves. However, I know without a shadow of doubt that he was the one who took away our beloved from us. I am aware that I should no longer stay in this place full of sinners but I cannot leave the boy alone. If he's allowed to grow in his father's shadow, he too will become like him.
