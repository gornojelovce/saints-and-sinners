You are a junior criminal investigator working for the La Cascada Detective Agency. Your agency has recieved a very difficult case and solving this case would mean a generous promotion for you. Your boss has told you:

" Find the killer."

You may take help from the following people to collect evidence and solve this case.

  • Librarian - newspapers and historical records.
  • Chief of Police - police report and testimonies of suspects
  • Friend at General Informations - call records and letters
  • Head Engineer - maps and CCTV footages of crime scenes and other places.
  • Ex Investigator - personal histories of important people and hints.

Good Luck to you!
