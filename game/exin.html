Personal Histories

  • Anton Liebert

    Born: 1910, Germany
    Died: 1969, La Cascada, US
    Anton Liebert became a priest after completing his studies in 1934. He emigrated to the states after the rise of Hitler in Germany. He travelled throughout the country, helping people where ever he could. He eventually settled in La Cascada and started his own church. He encouraged his church members to adopts children orphaned by wars. (The records of the children adopted by Liebert's church members can be found in the library)
    In the 50s and 60s he started job programs and rehabilations programs for young people suffering with drug and gambling addictions. He gained a reputations as the hero of the crime-ridden La Casada, but was also targeted by organised crime syndicates. He was stabbed to death on 4th November 1969 by a drug addict. (A news article on his death can be found in the library)

  • Carlos Ricardo Martinéz

    Ethinicity: Italian-Hispanic
    Physical Appearance: Short, scrawny but athletic
    Carlos Martinéz is the illegitimate son of Giovanni Marconi, born to a mother of Latino descent in 1960. He was raised by his mother alone until 1970 when she passed away from illness. Carlos soon turned to petty theft and crime. He was arrested multiple times between 1970 and 1980 for small crimes, but is respected in his local community as a Robin Hood figure. He has a strained relation with his father and half-brother Marco Romano, having renounced the name Ricardo given to him by his father.

  • Connor Quinn

    Born: 1943
    Ethinicity: Irish-American
    Physical Appearance: Tall and muscular
    Connor Quinn started his carrer by working odd jobs for Irish-American business owners in La Cascada in 1960. He started his own management agency in 1964 managing local restaurants and cafés which led him to getting into conflict with Leonardo Romano, younger brother of Giovanni Romano, who ran his own restaurant and refused to partner with Quinn's agency. He was deeply affected by Anton Liebert's death in 1969, and began funding his church ever since. In 1970, he failed trying to enter the casino business of La Cascada which was run entirely by the Romano family. (A news article on this can be found in the library)
    In 1982, he saw another confrontation with the Romanos when Tina Chase's café, which Quinn helped her set up, was being threated by Loenardo Romano, who ran his restaurant in the same area.

  • Giovanni Romano

    Born: 1936
    Ethinicity: Italian
    Physical Appearance: Tall and muscular but uses a cane to walk
    Current head of the Romano family which manages the casino business in La Cascada. He lost his wife in 1970, six years after the birth of his only son, Marco Romano. He hired several caretakers for his son. After the death of Anton Liebert in 1969, he publically expressed grief. He was widely criticised for it, as his casino business was one of the things Father Liebert fought vehemently against. He is also suspected of running a crime syndicate but law enforcement has been unable to find evidence against him.
    In 1982, he hired the medical professional Dr. Xingke Lee, for his back problems. Two years after that, he kicked his son Marco out due to his drug addiction and delinquent behaviour. Towards the end of 1985, he hired Dr. Lee again.

  • Leonardo Romano

    Born: 1940
    Ethinicity: Italian
    Physical Appearance: Tall and obese
    Younger brother of Giovanni Romano, head of the Romano family. In 1960, he separated from family business and started his own restaurant. When Connor Quinn started his management agency in 1965, Leonardo opposed his interference in his restaurant business. His restaurant was protected form Quinn by the help of the Romano family. He again found himself face-to-face with Quinn in 1981, when Quinn's acquaintance Tina Chase started her own café near Leonardo's restaurant.

  • Marco Romano

    Born: 18 August 1964
    Died: 23 March 1986
    Ethinicity: Italian
    The only (legitimate) son of Giovanni Romano, head of Romano family, Marco lost his mother at the age of six. Subsequently his father hired several caretakers for him. By 1975, Miki Anslow remained the sole caretaker of Marco. He started dating Gracia Lopéz in 1982 but they broke up in 1985 which Gracia claimed was due to his increasing abusive and violent behaviour. He was kicked out by his father in 1984 after becoming addicted to drugs and began living in a rented house with his caretaker Miki Anslow. (A news article on this can be found in the library.)
    He was found dead in Tina Chase's café on 23 March 1986. On January 21 of this year, he had been dragged out of the café by police after Tina Chase complained about him being a nuisance in his café multiple times. (A news article on this can be found in the library.)

  • Miki Anslow

    Born: 1935
    Ethinicity: Causasian
    Physcial Appearance: Cat-like eyes
    Miki was adopted by a Japanese family after being abandoned by her parents at birth. She grew up as Miki Hanazawa before her adoptive parents' death in 1946. She and her adoptive brother were saved by Father Anton Liebert, who bought them to his church where they were adopted by an Anslow couple. In 1963, she started her training to be a nun. In 1970, after her brother went missing, she was hired by Giovanni Romano as one of the caretakers for his son Marco Romano. By 1975, she remained the sole caretaker of Giovanni's son. She is know as a sweet and kind lady, well respected by one and all in La Cascada. Some see her as the spititual successor of Father Liebert. (The records of the children adopted by Liebert's church members can be found in the library)

  • Ryuji Anslow

    Born: 1941
    Current Status: Unknown
    Ethinicity: Japanese
    Physical Appearance: tall and muscular
    Ryuji was born to the Hanazawa couple as the younger brother of his adoptive sister Miki. They lost their parents in 1946 and were adopted by Anslows, who were members of Anton Liebert's church. In 1966, he joined the police force and named Father Liebert as his inspiration for helping people. In 1970, less than a year after Father Liebert's death, he went missing while investigating a case. Since then, no one has heard anything of him and he is officially assumed dead. (The records of the children adopted by Liebert's church members can be found in the library)

  • Xingke Lee

    Born: 1952
    Ethinicity: Chinese
    Physical Appearance: slender and athletic, sports long hair
    Not much is known of his childhood. He was found by Father Anton Liebert in 1962 who saved him from some criminals. He was given a place in La Cascada after a couple who were members of Liebert's church adopted him. After Liebert's death in 1969, he started studying medicine to honour the man who saved him by dedicaing his life to saving people. He completed his studies by 1976 and went to the East to practice medicine. He returned to the States in 1982. Soon he was hired by Giovanni Romano for his back problems. He alternated between living in the Romano household and going on trips across the country for a couple of years. Towards the end of 1985, he was again hired by Giovanni but was soon relieved of his work in the 2nd month of 1986. (The records of the children adopted by Liebert's church members can be found in the library)

Hints

  1. Have you read today's newspaper?
  2. If someone seems suspicious, check their call records and letters.
  3. Remember to read people's personal histories. They can give you an idea of their motives. Also remember their physical appearance so you can identify them on footage.
  4. Check testimonies against each other and with CCTV footage to distinguish truth from lies.
  5. Don't forget to read the police report of the murder.
  6. Keep track of the timeline of events.
  7. Check the maps before seeing CCTV footage so you know which cameras to check.
  8. If someone mentions that they went to a place or were somewhere at a certin time, it is a good idea to confirm that with the CCTV footage.
