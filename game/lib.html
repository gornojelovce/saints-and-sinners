Newspapers

  • 1986 March 24

    SON OF BUSINESS TYCOON FOUND MURDERED IN CAFÉ

    Marco Romano, son of businessman Giovanni Romano, was found dead in Tina Chase's café. Initial police report suggest he was shot in the head last night and then his body was dragged into the café which was then locked from outside. His body was found by the owner of the café next morning. Police has questioned multiple people involved with the murdered individual. These include victim's father Giovanni Romano, ex-girlfriend Gracia Lopéz, caretaker Miki Anslow, café's owner Tina Chase and Giovanni's business rival Connor Quinn, among others, yet the chief suspect remains known criminal Carloz Martinéz, half-brother to Marco Romano.

  • 1986 January 21

    LOCAL CAFÉ OWNER CALLS POLICE ON GIOVANNI ROMANO'S SON

    Marco Romano, son of Giovanni Romano, again got into trouble with the law. This time, he got into fights with the customers of a humble café owned by Mrs. Tina Chase. Mrs. Chase called the cops on him which were only sent back once Marco's caretaker, Mrs. Miki Anslow begged Mrs. Chase to forgive Marco. Had Mrs. Chase refused, the footage of the surveillance camera would have been enough evidence to incriminate Marco on charge of publc misdemeanor.

  • 1984 May 16

    ONLY SON KICKED OUT BY LARGEST CASINO OWNER OF LA CASCADA

    Giovanni Romano, head of the Romano family, has removed his son, Marco Romano from his house. This is likely in response to his unsigthly behaviour as of late. Twenty year old Marco has repeatedly gotten in trouble with law enforcement agencies due to his drug abuse and involvement with biker gangs. Mr. Romano has said about his son, I'll only allow him back in my house once he return to the right path and starts behaving in the manner suitable for the son of Giovanni Romano.

  • 1970 August 7

    QUINN VS ROMANO - WHO WILL BE THE KING OF CASINOS

    Connor Quinn, who has been managing the restaurants and cafés of La Cascada for the past 6 years is now setting foot in casino business. The Romano family has been the largest player in casino business since four decades and are unlikely to let Quinn run his own casinos. Mr. Giovanni Romano has called Quinn's plans nice dreams. Mr. Quinn's decision has come as a surprise to many, considering he spoke greatly of Father Liebert and his war on gamling, not even a year ago.

  • 1969 November 4

    LA CASCADA LOST ITS SAINT

    Father Anton Liebert, who dedicated his life to the service of the people of La Cascada, was stabbed to death by a drug addict near his church, this morning. Police suspect deeper conspiracy and mafia involvement in the murder bacause of Father Liebert's vocal campaign against drugs and gambling. The infamous Romano family which runs the casinos in La Cascada, had come under the radar.
    The murderer was apprehended on site and arrested. He denies any involvement with any criminal organisations and denies any motive behind his actions, blaming his intoxicated state. Condolences are pouring in from all over the state and a funeral will be arranged for Father Liebert a week from now.
