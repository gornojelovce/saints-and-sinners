Police Report

  • Police report on Marco's murder

    Name: Marco Romano
    Age: 22
    Cause of Death: Gunshot to head
    Body Found: March 24, 1986, 6:45 am by Tina Chase in her café
    Estimated Time of Death: between 10:00 pm and 11:00 pm on March 23, 1986
    Estimated Place of Murder: The alley behind or on the side of the café
    Murderer: Unknown
    Current working theory: Marco was shot outside the café and then brought inside where he was eventually found. No blood was found anywhere except in the kitchen where the body was. There is no sign of dragging which suggests the victim was carried inside by the murdered. No fingerprints or signs of struggle were found inside the café. The café was locked from outside in the morning according to the owner. If she is to be believed then the killer must have possessed a key. The killer is likely a professional. The victim seems to have gotten into a fight shortly before the murder but that may or may not be realted to the murder.
    More details will likely come out after analysing the footages of the cameras inside and near the café.

Testimonies

  • Carlos Ricardo Martinéz

    Q. This is not the first time we are meeting Martinéz. You've always been a lowlife theif. Did you murder your brother?
    A. No, officer. I did not.
    Q. That's it? You don't have anything more to say? When was the last time you met him?
    A. I don't remember.
    Q. Don't lie to me. You met him right?
    A. Yeah I did. I met him. So what?
    Q. When and where did you meet him?
    A. Last night at 10.
    Q. And where?
    A. ...
    Q. You won't talk?
    A. ... I did not kill him. I won't say anymore.
    Q. Gracia Lopéz is a friend of yours?
    A. Yes. She is.
    Q. Was she involved in this?
    A. She's not involved in anything. Neither am I.
    Q. Fine then. If you don't want to tell us, we will ask Gracia.

  • Connor Quinn

    Q. Mr. Connor Quinn. You agency manages the café where the murder took place, right?
    A. Yes.
    Q. And you also had a history with the uncle of the victim.
    A. What happened between me and Leonardo Romano is unrelated to his nephew Marco Romano.
    Q. Would you tell me about your and Ms. Chase's relation?
    A. Tina was the sister of my employee. Her brother told me to take care of her shortly before he passed away. Ever since I've cared for her like my own sister.
    Q. Marco has bothered her multiple times in the past. Have you ever though of doing something about the man bothering your "sister"?
    A. The Romanos have always been trouble for me and this city. Even if I wanted to do something, I wouldn't take the law in my own hands. I'm not like those lawless bastards. Why don't you question his own father or his delinquent brother. There's already a lot of hostility in the family.
    Q. Could you have done this to get revenge on Leonardo Romano?
    A. I would be a fool to kill his nephew on my own property. Go ask Leonardo, he might have done this to frame me. Everyone knows he has no love for his family and he's a scoundrel all and all.
    Q. Final question, Mr. Quinn. Where were you last night at 10?
    A. I was in my hotel last night, sitting in the cafeteria by the window with a .... friend ... of mine, sipping coffee, I think. You can check the footage if you like.

  • Giovanni Romano

    Q. Mr. Romano, I'm sorry for your loss. Is there something you want to tell up about your son?
    A. I should have kept him with me. I shouldn't have left him alone. It's my fault.
    Q. Is there someone you suspect?
    A. I don't know. Maybe someone held a grudge against him or me.
    Q. Is there a possibility you were involved in this. Marco was ruining your image after all with all his recent actions? Or is it because of your illegitimate son Carlos?
    A. Mind your words, officer. I might have been hard on my son but that's because I truly cared for him. And how would harming one of my son help the other?
    Q. Okay then... you've recently hired Dr. Xingke Lee after kicking out Marco. You also hired him a few years ago when he first returned from abroad. Some people belive him to be shady person. And furthermore, he stopped working for you shortly before yesterday's events? Is this all a coincidence?
    A. Dr. Lee was recommended to me by a trusted employee of mine. He is a skilled doctor and that is the only reason I employed him. To take care of my health and my family's health. This is unrelated to anything that happened last night.
    Q. Thank you for your cooperation Mr. Romano. We will ask the rest of the qustions from Dr. Lee himself.

  • Gracia Lopéz

    Q. Ms. Gracia Lopéz, you and Marco were together for how long?
    A. 3 years.
    Q. You broke up last year right? What was the reason.
    A. Marco... he was abusive. So I broke up with him.
    Q. Didn't that lead to a fight between him and his brother Carlos?.
    A. I was afraid Marco would react badly so I called Carl in case thing didn't go well. But they ended up fighting.
    Q. What is your relation with Carlos Martinéz?
    A. He is a good friend of mine. I met him some time after I started dating Marco. But Marco didn't like him so he told me to stay away from Carl.
    Q. Did Carlos meet Marco recently?
    A. Yes. Last night at 10. Marco called me to meet him near Tina's cafe. His caretaker Ms. Anslow told him to. I didn't tell Marco. But he found out somehow.
    Q. So he came there and killed Marco?
    A. No! He didn't kill Marco. We left him there. And we don't know what happened after that.

  • Leonardo Romano

    Q. Mr. Leonardo Romano, do you suspect anyone of killing your nephew?
    A. Any number of good and bad folks might have done it. He was not the most loved person here, you know.
    Q. Mr. Romano, you don't seem sad at your nephew's death? Mr. Quinn suspects that you might have done it to get revenge on him? Or could it be to get you own children to inherit the Romano fortune?
    A. My brother, his son, they are all the same. Not one worth crying over. I don't want my kids to near them and their dirty money. And that arrogant Quinn is not deserving of my revenge either.
    Q. Where were you last night at 10, Mr. Romano?
    A. Where was I? Where would I be? I was in my restaurant, making some honest money. Investigate all you want, you won't find any dirt on me.

  • Miki Anslow

    Q. Ms. Miki Anslow. What was your relation to the victim, Marco Romano?
    A. Marco was like a son to me. I have raised him ever since his mother passed away.
    Q. His death must have caused you grief. Is there someone you suspect?
    A. Losing my father, brother and now a son, grief is all I've ever known. The greatest grief I have is that I failed to raise him properly. He was ... rude to everyone. But I don't know anyone who would do this.
    Q. Ever since he was kicked out by his father, you've been with him. Why did he left the house last night?
    A. To apologise to Gracia. I told him to go and say sorry to her for his behaviour towards her. But I didn't knew something like this would happen.
    Q. Do you suspect Gracia or Marco's brother Carlos?
    A. No! They are both very sweet children. Yes, Gracia suffered because of Marco and Carlos and him got into fights all the time, but they would never do something like this.
    Q. But Carlos has been involved in criminal activities, you are aware right?
    A. Yes I know. He was abandoned by his father and lost his mother at a young age. I can fell his pain having experienced both abandonement and losing your parents. He is a product of his circumstances, he is not bad by nature.
    Q. Have you contacted the Romano family since Marco was kicked out?
    A. Yes. I went to the Romano mansion on 4th of February to talk to Mr. Romano about Marco.

  • Tina Chase

    Q. Ms. Tina Chase. You are the owner of the café where the body was found and you were the one who reported to police, is that right?
    A. Yes. When I went to the cafe this morning I saw blood coming out of kitchen and rushed in to find Marco Romano dead.
    Q. The café was locked from outside right?
    A. Yes it was.
    Q. Does anyone else possess the key to the café or did you gave it to someone?
    A. No there's only one key and I'm sure I locked it last night so no one should've been able to enter either.
    Q. What time did you leave yesterday?
    A. I left early last night at 9:30 because Mr. Quinn called and told me I was to recieve a shipment.
    Q. Mr. Quinn? He's the one whose agency manages most restaurants and cafés in that area right? Could be be involved in the murder? He has had a history with the Romanos after all.
    A. No. Mr. Quinn is a very kind man. He helped me after my brother passed away. He would never do something like that.
    Q. This Marco fellow. You've once called police on him? It was in the newspapers.
    A. That's right, two months ago on 21, he was bothering my customers so I called the police.

  • Xingke Lee

    Q. Dr. Shing...kay? Lee? Is that how it's pronounced?
    A. More or less, yes.
    Q. Dr. Lee, you were hired by Mr. Giovanni Romano some time after he kicked Marco out and he ended your service little more than a month ago. Are you in any form involved with the murder of Marco Romano? What sort of work did you do for Giovanni Romano?
    A. No. I'm not involved. It came to me as a shock too. Mr. Romano hired me for his back problems. I have treated other members of his family as well. Some time ago Mr. Romano told me on call his back is much better now and my services are no longer needed. I left shortly after.
    Q. Where were you last night, Mr. Lee?
    A. I was with a client of mine.
    Q. Can I ask who?
    A. I'm sorry, as a doctor I cannot disclose information about my clients without their permission.
